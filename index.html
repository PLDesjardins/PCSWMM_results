Know your numbers. Peak flow is Maximum Total Inflow, in the report’s flow units. Total inflow is cumulative Total Inflow Volume. Liter volumes are converted to m³: 1,000 liters = 1 m³, and 1 million liters = 1,000 m³. Results use the report’s printed precision.
